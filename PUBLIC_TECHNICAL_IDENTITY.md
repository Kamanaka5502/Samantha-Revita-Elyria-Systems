## AI Systems Engineer · Founder & Lead Architect, Elyria Systems

Samantha Revita-Wagner designs proof-bearing execution-governance systems for AI agents, automation, and other consequence-bearing software.

Her work focuses on the boundary immediately before proposed machine action becomes protected consequence:

The engineering question is not merely whether a model can produce an output. It is whether a proposed movement still has current standing, valid authority, sufficient evidence, preserved continuity, and admissible effect custody at the exact point where consequence could bind.

## Public engineering thesis

```text
capability != standing
standing != authority
authority signature != authority consumption
authority consumption != observed effect
observed effect != committed protected consequence
```

A decision record is not sufficient evidence that an unsafe effect was prevented. Elyria Systems therefore treats the proposed movement itself as the governed object and requires explicit admission before protected consequence may bind.

## What an external reviewer should try to break

Do not evaluate the work by terminology alone.

Change the authority. Break standing. Corrupt evidence. Replay a consumed token. Alter the route. Force a prohibited state. Race the boundary. Restart persistence. Attempt to make an inadmissible movement create protected consequence.

If prohibited movement can still bind, the bounded claim fails.

If it cannot bind under the declared attack surface and frozen invariant set, that result is evidence for that specific claim—not universal certification.

## Evidence discipline

**Where the evidence stops, the claim stops.**
